GLOBAL SENTIMENT TRAJECTORY OBSERVATIONS:
- Early Stage: Curiosity-driven positivity
- Mid Stage: Habit formation + gamification
- Later Stage: Reflection, reduced novelty
- Long-Term: Advocacy, expectation of depth and transparency

Motivation → Routine → Ownership → Advocacy

Feature Sentiment Over Time:
- Trackers: Positive → Strongly Positive → Stable
